# Technical Focus

## Overview

My primary technical focus is on building end-to-end, production-oriented AI systems rather than working on isolated models or purely experimental prototypes. I am especially interested in the engineering of intelligent systems that integrate data pipelines, retrieval systems, model orchestration, backend services, and user-facing applications into reliable, usable products.

Rather than treating machine learning models as standalone components, I focus on the full system surrounding them, including how data is ingested, processed, retrieved, and served to users in real applications.

## End-to-End AI Systems

I am particularly interested in designing and building complete AI systems that include:

- Data ingestion and preprocessing pipelines  
- Document processing and chunking strategies  
- Embedding generation and vector storage  
- Retrieval and re-ranking pipelines  
- Prompt orchestration and tool usage  
- Backend APIs and service integration  
- User-facing application layers  

I care strongly about how these components work together as a coherent system rather than as isolated pieces.

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

A major area of focus in my work is retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and knowledge-based AI systems.

My interests in this area include:

- Designing document ingestion pipelines  
- Choosing and evaluating chunking strategies  
- Working with embeddings and vector databases  
- Building retrieval pipelines for relevant context selection  
- Prompt composition and orchestration  
- Evaluating answer quality, grounding, and reliability  

I am particularly interested in how large language models can be combined with external knowledge sources and tools to create more reliable, controllable, and useful systems.

I enjoy thinking about how to design AI systems that are not only intelligent, but also dependable and practical in production settings.

My goal is to become an AI engineer who can design, build, and maintain complete intelligent systems rather than only individual model components.

## Direction of Growth

Going forward, I want to continue deepening my experience in:

- Building larger and more AI systems  
- Backend engineering for AI products  
- Service-oriented and modular system design  
- Evaluation and reliability of AI systems  
- MLOps and deployment-oriented workflows  

My technical direction is centered on becoming a strong AI systems engineer focused on real-world, production-grade intelligent applications.
