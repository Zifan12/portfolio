# Career Goals

## Overview

My career goal is to build a long-term career as an AI engineer focused on designing, building, and deploying real-world, production-grade intelligent systems. I am most interested in applied machine learning and AI engineering roles that involve working on full systems.

I want to work on products and platforms where machine learning, software engineering, and system design come together to solve practical, real-world problems.

## Short-Term Goals

In the near term, my primary goals are:

- To obtain internships or applied engineering roles where I can gain hands-on experience with real-world machine learning systems and AI-powered products
- To work on production-oriented codebases involving data pipelines, backend services, and AI integration
- To learn how real engineering teams design, build, test, deploy, and maintain AI systems
- To deepen my practical experience with backend development, system design, and applied machine learning

## Long-Term Goals

In the long term, I aim to:

- Work as an AI engineer on large-scale, real-world intelligent systems
- Build systems that integrate machine learning models, retrieval systems, data pipelines, and software infrastructure into reliable products
- Take ownership of end-to-end AI systems, from data ingestion and model integration to deployment, evaluation, and product integration
- Contribute to products that are used by real users and solve meaningful problems in the real world.

I am especially interested in roles that sit at the intersection of AI, software engineering, and systems engineering.

## Type of Work I Am Most Interested In

I am most interested in work that involves:

- Building end-to-end AI systems
- Designing and maintaining RAG and knowledge-based systems
- Backend and infrastructure engineering for AI products
- System design, reliability, and scalability
- Turning research ideas and models into usable, production-ready systems

## Professional Direction

Overall, my professional direction is to grow into a strong AI systems engineer who is capable of designing, building, and maintaining complex intelligent systems in real-world production environments, rather than focusing purely on experimental work.
